# Note on the back office and the checkout pages: they are kept out of
# search by a <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> in each page, NOT by
# a Disallow here. Those two things pull against each other — Disallow
# stops a crawler fetching the page, so it never reads the noindex, and
# the bare URL can still surface in results with no snippet. Letting them
# be fetched is what makes the noindex actually work.
