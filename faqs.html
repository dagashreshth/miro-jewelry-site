Product & Certification

Hallmarks, certificates and how to read them.

Every gram of our gold is BIS-hallmarked — the purity mark and unique HUID are laser-etched discreetly on each piece. Diamonds are independently graded by IGI, and the certificate detailing cut, colour, clarity and carat travels in your order beside the invoice. Keep the two together; they matter for insurance, valuation and any future resale.

Nothing your eye, a loupe or a tester can find — both are pure crystallised carbon with identical fire and hardness. A natural diamond formed underground over a billion years or more; a lab-grown one crystallises over weeks in controlled conditions, is graded to the same 4C standards with an IGI certificate, and costs meaningfully less carat for carat. We work with both, and every product page states plainly which stone you're looking at.

Measure the inner diameter of a ring that already fits and match it to the size guide on any ring's product page — we use standard Indian sizes 6 through 18. If you're between sizes, choose the larger; your first resize is complimentary within 15 days, so a near-miss is never a problem. For milestone pieces, come and be sized properly at the atelier.

Most rings, coins and disc pendants take hand engraving of up to 15 characters — initials, a date, coordinates, one private word. Add it in the customisation step on the product page and we'll cut it at the bench before dispatch. One thing to know: engraved pieces are yours alone, so they sit outside the 15-day return policy.

Jewelry Care

Small rituals that keep gold golden and pearls alive.

Make jewelry the last thing you put on and the first thing you take off — perfume, hairspray and sanitiser are what dull gold and film over stones. After wear, wipe with the soft cloth from your Miró box and rest the piece in its own storage pouch, away from neighbours that could scratch it. A monthly bath in lukewarm water with a drop of mild soap and a soft brush keeps pavé lively.

Lifetime care, in a word: complimentary cleaning, polishing and rhodium or gold plating touch-ups for as long as you own the piece. Book over WhatsApp or leave it with us at the Kala Ghoda atelier — most refreshes are ready within the week. We also recommend a yearly prong check for daily-worn rings; that's part of the same promise.

One piece, one pouch — gold will scratch gold, and diamonds will scratch everything. Fasten chains before they're put away to spare yourself the knots, and keep pearls apart from metals entirely. When travelling, the pouches from your order are the safest home, and fine jewelry belongs in hand luggage, never checked.
