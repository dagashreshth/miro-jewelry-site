Our world

A young house with
old-world hands

Miró was founded in 2025 with a simple conviction: the jewelry worn by modern Indian women should be designed with the same artistry it's crafted with. We work with third-generation setters and karigars across Mumbai and Jaipur, pairing their hands with a design language that is clean, editorial and quietly cool.

Every stone is certified, every gram of gold is BIS-hallmarked, and every silhouette is tested against one question — will her daughter fight to inherit it?
